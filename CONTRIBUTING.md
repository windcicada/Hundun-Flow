# Contributing to HUNDUN-FLOW

Contributions must have a known, documented origin and must be submitted under
terms compatible with the Apache License 2.0. Contributions of uncertain origin
are rejected, including code, tests, documentation, data, and generated content.

Every contribution must certify the Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 in
[`DCO.md`](DCO.md). Every commit must include a sign-off in this form:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
```

Create the sign-off with `git commit -s`. By adding it, the contributor certifies
that the contribution satisfies the DCO and that they have the right to submit it.
