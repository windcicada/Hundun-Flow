## Target-series identity

The Fig. 9 legend defines filled circles as the present PIV experiments and
combines cases 1 and 2 in that legend entry.  Marker shape alone therefore
does not establish a case number.  The article body states that the
statistical profiles presented thereafter come from case 2, and repeats this
immediately before Figs. 11--15.  Figures 11--15 are those subsequent
statistical profiles.  The frozen target is consequently the filled circular
present-PIV series in each panel, with `PIV case 2` supplied by the article's
scope statement.  Crosses, open markers, LES lines, and all prior-study lines
are excluded.

Pages 11--12 contain no embedded raster image objects.  The filled PIV marker
is an individual black circular vector object with nominal diameter about
`1.078` PDF points.  Line vertices are strokes and crosses are separate
stroke paths, so neither is interchangeable with the filled objects.

## Frozen object grouping

Within each panel, select only complete filled circular objects of the target
style and diameter.  Preserve PDF/SVG document order.  Each station series is
drawn as a monotone decreasing-abscissa object sequence; a positive abscissa
jump greater than one quarter of the panel width starts the next sequence.
The extraction must yield exactly three sequences.  Sequence order is
`x/D = 1.06`, `1.54`, `2.02`, independently checked by the vertical ordering
and station labels.  Within each sequence, reverse to increasing `y/D` before
export.  Objects clipped by the panel boundary or obscured in the published
artwork are not reconstructed.
