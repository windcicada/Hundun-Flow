# Parnaudeau Fig. 11--15 controlled-digitization rule

Status: `FROZEN_PRE_EXTRACTION_R3`

Revision history: R1 was frozen at commit `73cebbf` before a source artifact
had been acquired.  R2 is frozen after main-agent inspection established that
the admissible institutional PDF contains vector marker objects, and before
any profile array or HUNDUN long-statistics output exists.  R2 retains the R1
raster route for raster-only artwork and adds the exact-vector route below.
R2's first development extraction was rejected by independent audit B because
the implementation synthesized evenly spaced tick coordinates from panel
boxes and did not fail-close a common-mode marker insertion or deletion. No
R2 array is an authority. R3 freezes the actual-tick and canonical-inventory
requirements below before any replacement authoritative extraction and before
any HUNDUN long-statistics output.

This rule is frozen before any HUNDUN long-statistics output exists. It applies
only after the main agent accepts an actually acquired publisher VOR,
author-uploaded primary PDF/figure, institution-hosted primary PDF/figure, or
author array artifact. A DOI page, abstract, preview, screenshot, secondary
CFD reproduction, access-denied body or download pointer is not a source
artifact.

## Required authority matrix

The completed reference shall contain exactly 15 profiles: each of
`x/D = 1.06, 1.54, 2.02` crossed with Fig. 11 mean `u/Uc`, Fig. 12 mean
`v/Uc`, Fig. 13 `u'u'/Uc^2`, Fig. 14 `v'v'/Uc^2`, and Fig. 15
`u'v'/Uc^2`. Every profile shall identify the printed page, figure/panel,
station, full legend entry and target `present PIV` case. LES and other
experimental or numerical curves in the same panel are excluded.

Author arrays take precedence. They require field definitions, units or
normalization, coordinate origin, station mapping, version/date, stable public
provenance, exact bytes and SHA-256. If those fields are absent, the arrays do
not bypass controlled digitization.

## Acquisition and rendering

Record the public URL, redirect chain and response headers, UTC acquisition
time, byte count, SHA-256, MIME/magic, PDF metadata, page count and the output
of `pdfimages -list` or an equivalent page-object inventory. The original
artifact remains unchanged outside Git and is bound as a literature-receipt
attachment.

For vector artwork, perform two cleared conversions from the unchanged PDF to
SVG/page coordinates and retain both complete conversion logs and outputs.
Canonical marker-object inventories from the two conversions must be exactly
equal; disagreement rejects the source/converter pair.  Also render every
complete panel, with its axes and station labels, independently at 1200 and
2400 dpi without sharpening, denoising or geometric correction and confirm
every retained marker identity and panel boundary in both renders.  The crop
geometry is derived from and recorded in full-page coordinates; a panel render
is not used to recalibrate an axis.  These renders are independent
visual/object-presence audits;
they do not replace the vector coordinates with raster estimates.  For
embedded raster artwork,
extract the original image when possible and record its native resolution;
upsampling does not create additional authority. A trace that changes curve
identity between renderings is rejected.

## Panel calibration and sampling

Each panel and each independent extraction uses its own calibration. For each
axis, fit the affine map `z = a p + b` from at least three printed tick centres
by least squares, where `p` is the vector page coordinate for the exact-vector
route or the raster pixel coordinate for the raster route, and `z` is the
printed data coordinate. Record every tick coordinate/data pair, fitted coefficients and
the maximum tick residual

```text
r_z = max_i |a p_i + b - z_i|.
```

Fewer than three unambiguous ticks on either axis rejects the panel. A
calibration from another panel, a cropped preview, an inferred zero or an
assumed shared offset is forbidden.  If a composite panel deliberately shifts
the three station profiles, the source/layout freeze must preregister every
station's additive presentation offset from printed baselines before profile
extraction.  Store both the printed ordinate and the recovered physical value;
silently applying or inferring an offset during extraction is forbidden.

For the exact-vector route, the tick centres must be parsed from the actual
major-tick segments in the source SVG axis/frame path. The raw trace records
the complete tick coordinates, path ordinal, path-element SHA-256, `d`
attribute SHA-256, style and transform for both cleared conversions. Panel-box
endpoints or evenly spaced coordinates generated from a panel box are locators,
not measurements, and are forbidden calibration inputs. The two tick
inventories must be exactly equal before either is mapped.

Digitize only the centres of published target PIV markers. Do not densify a
line, smooth points, impose symmetry, reflect one half-profile, or fill an
occluded marker by interpolation. Each profile needs at least eight visible,
ordered points. Symmetry/antisymmetry is an audit diagnostic only. The common
`y/D` interval is the intersection actually covered by all 15 retained
profiles; every profile must span the declared interval. Station substitution
or streamwise interpolation is forbidden.

## Exact-vector selection and extraction-error bound

For the exact-vector route, retain only filled circular marker objects whose
complete bounding boxes lie inside the preregistered panel box and whose
diameter and fill style match the target legend glyph.  Text glyphs, crosses,
open markers, line vertices and circular objects outside the panel are
excluded.  Preserve source document order.  A station sequence boundary must
be declared in the source/layout freeze and independently recover exactly the
registered three sequences; manual point insertion or deletion is forbidden.
The clean source's per-sequence cardinalities and canonical raw marker-object
inventory SHA-256 are frozen in the source/layout document. Both are mandatory
checks in each extraction pass. Equality between two conversions alone is not
sufficient: any duplicate, deletion, style change, boundary shift or ordering
change must be rejected against that frozen clean-source baseline.

Run the exact selection twice from cleared conversion directories.  One pass
is reviewed against the 1200-dpi rendering and the other against the 2400-dpi
rendering by independent operators.  The canonical raw object inventories and
mapped values must agree exactly.  This is a deterministic vector repeat, not
a claim of two statistically independent measurements.

For vector axis `z`, let `q_k` be half the maximum decimal quantization step of
the converted page coordinate and `w_k` the full printed marker width in page
coordinates.  The conservative bound is

```text
B_z,k = r_z,k + |a_z,k| (q_k + w_k/2).
```

The retained value is the common value from the two equal canonical
inventories, and its controlled-digitization bound is

```text
E_z = max(B_z,1, B_z,2).
```

If the exact inventories disagree, no midpoint is formed and the extraction
is rejected.

## Raster-only independent repeat and extraction-error bound

For artwork without extractable vector marker objects, two traces are
performed from cleared calibration/selection state. Prefer two
independent operators; if only one operator is available, the second pass uses
the alternate rendering and is performed without loading the first pass's
pixel selections. Raw tick and marker pixel coordinates from both passes are
retained.

For axis `z` in trace `k`, let `w_k` be the measured full marker/line width in
pixels at the picked point. The conservative single-trace bound is

```text
B_z,k = r_z,k + |a_z,k| (0.5 + w_k/2).
```

The retained value is the arithmetic midpoint of the two independently
mapped values. Its controlled-digitization bound is

```text
E_z = max(B_z,1, B_z,2) + |z_1 - z_2|/2.
```

Compute and record this bound separately for `y/D` and the profile value at
every point. A profile-level `coordinate_absolute` and `value_absolute` is the
maximum pointwise bound over that profile. This is a conservative L1-style
bound: tick-fit residual, half-pixel quantization, half marker/line width and
repeat disagreement are not combined by RSS. If page distortion, overlapping
curves, missing markers or legend ambiguity is not covered by these recorded
terms, reject the affected profile rather than inflating an undocumented
tolerance.

The paper reports no pointwise PIV uncertainty for these curves. Extraction
error remains labelled `not_reported_separately` from experimental
uncertainty; the paper's LES sampling estimate and HWA uncertainty are never
reused as PIV error.

## Bound artifacts and gate rule

Controlled digitization binds five named, distinct receipt-attachment roles:

1. `source_primary`: the unchanged primary source artifact;
2. `rule`: this frozen rule document;
3. `calibration`: machine-readable acquisition, page, panel and axis records;
4. `raw_trace`: both passes' unmodified tick/marker vector-object inventories
   or raster pixel selections;
5. `extraction_script`: the exact deterministic mapping/export program.

The reference names every role and SHA-256, and the machine receipt binds each
artifact. Changing the method requires a new preregistered rule before any
replacement trace.

An author-array completion instead binds three distinct named roles:
`source_author_arrays`, this `rule`, and the author's `field_dictionary` that
defines coordinates, stations, normalization and quantities. It may mark the
pixel-calibration method fields not applicable, but it may not omit numerical
precision/error metadata or any of the 15 profiles.

The completed reference embeds the 15 ordered numerical arrays and their
profile-level bounds. `tools/v04_literature_extract.py` independently rejects
missing/duplicate matrix rows, fewer than eight points, non-increasing `y/D`,
missing common coverage, incomplete method/locator/error fields, source/hash
drift and unbound attachments. Only a newly generated receipt that passes
`receipt-validate --require-complete` may remove the full20/long-statistics
stop.

This rule does not choose the later physical-accuracy norm or tolerance, does
not authorize full20, and does not decide the literature or final gate.
