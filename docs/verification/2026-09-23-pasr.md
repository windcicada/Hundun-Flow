# Single-field timescale PaSR

The reaction input now defaults to one thermochemical field and normalizes that
choice to the existing algebraic PaSR model. This is the requested physical-model
choice, not TCR and not a claim of equivalence to multi-field ESF. The closure,
input compatibility and COAST comparison are specified in [pasr.md](../pasr.md).

Two correctness failures were reproduced before changing their implementation:

- Pure-species Cantera mixtures can have zero mixture-averaged diffusion and a
  nonzero trace reaction rate. The old mixing-time calculation rejected these
  states with detail 10214 on initialization/restoration. The analytic infinite
  mixing-time limit now gives zero reacting fraction. A separate subnormal
  diffusion case retains a representable nonzero fraction without a floor.
- The CN/BE PaSR route omitted the conservative total-energy row and its
  independent composition ledger. A reacting 128-rank advance had a physical
  energy imbalance of 6.61356e-5, exceeding the unchanged 1e-6 audit threshold.
  PaSR now uses the common total-energy row and audits actual registered chemical
  sources and species face fluxes. It does not reconstruct budgets from a
  residual closure identity.

Focused validation passed:

- 27 production CLI input-selection checks, including equivalent canonical
  identities, invalid counts/options, multi-field identities, and native restart
  across equivalent single-field input spellings.
- Real-Cantera pure-species initialization, advance and native restart.
- Mixing-time and reacting-fraction unit tests, including zero diffusion,
  subnormal diffusion and the rejected undefined 0/0 limit.
- Existing case-compiler unit suite.
- Product reaction tests on MPI-1 and MPI-2, including the new conservative
  PaSR energy contract, independent composition budgets, zero-rate states and
  provider failure/rollback. MPI-2 PaSR SGS/retry coverage also passed.
- The corrected 128-rank real-geometry first advance took 17.4733 s, used six
  outer iterations and required no retry. Independent relative errors were
  9.45913e-13 for mass, 2.14298e-9 for physical energy, and at most 1.81252e-12
  for elements. Chemical conversion was nonzero. A separate native-checkpoint
  read verified both prescribed external inlet fluxes and zero unlabelled inflow.

Build scope: changed runtime translation units were rebuilt and linked over the
V1.1.0 archive; this is not a fresh full-repository build. Focused tests are
registered in CTest. Large-case inputs, geometry and generated results remain
local and are excluded from the repository. The first-step timing includes the
authorized change of physical model and cannot be attributed solely to kernel
optimization. Continuation passed the accepted-state budgets through step 14,
but encountered species target-solve rejection 10217 and time-step retries from
step 10. The remaining frozen-source/transport convergence issue is under
investigation; this change does not establish usable long-run throughput.
Larger-step stability, time refinement and long-run acceptance remain pending.
